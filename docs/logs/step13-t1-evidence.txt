### Why step 10 read "PUT DELETE" at crs-setup.conf lines 494/583

Fresh read of the pinned default container (6296ce68, no policy env,
DIAGNOSTIC, docs/logs/step13-t1-crs-setup-lines.txt): lines 494/583 are
COMMENTED example `setvar:'tx.allowed_methods=GET HEAD POST OPTIONS'` /
`tx.allowed_request_content_type=|…6 types…|` lines — no PUT/DELETE anywhere;
env shows ALLOWED_METHODS= (empty). The image ENV has no ALLOWED_METHODS
default (docker inspect: only CORS_HEADER_403_ALLOW_METHODS="GET, POST, PUT,
DELETE, OPTIONS", a different setting).

Step-11's T1 audit (docs/baseline-config.md "Step-11 T1" section) already
identified the source: the step-10 grep was executed against the container
that happened to be up at that moment — the last ALIGNED PL2 run, whose
ALLOWED_METHODS env was "GET HEAD POST OPTIONS PUT DELETE" — and its rendered
values were misattributed to the image default. The committed policy file
(docs/logs/wcp-heldout-policy.json) shows the step-10 "default_methods" list
was exactly that 6-method env value. The exact container of that grep cannot
be re-inspected today, so the attribution rests on the step-11 audit record
plus the fresh pinned-image read above; the superseding fact (true default =
GET HEAD POST OPTIONS via 901160/901162; PUT/DELETE blocked by 911100 in every
default run) is PROVEN by the behavioral signature check in all 12 runs.
