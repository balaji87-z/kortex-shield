== PROVEN (raw engine audit entries, one per carrier; see
docs/logs/step17-t1-audit-passed.txt for the passed samples and
docs/logs/step17-t1-audit-per-carrier.txt for blocked samples from the
committed s16-t2 run):

1. The engine transaction RECEIVES the body for text/plain,
   application/octet-stream and application/x-kortex-probe-unknown: the
   audit request.body field carries the raw body for every one of the three
   carriers (blocked samples from the committed s16-t2 run AND passed
   samples from the DIAGNOSTIC audit-always-on run).
2. The URLENCODED fallback processor IS engaged for those carriers: the
   diagnostic dump rule 999908 shows REQBODY_PROCESSOR == URLENCODED at
   phase 2 for text/plain, octet-stream and unknown-ct (engaged via CRS
   901340 ctl:forceRequestBodyVariable=On + coraza's rbp=="" -> URLENCODED
   fallback, coraza v3.7.0 transaction.go line ~1111). Control: the
   urlencoded carrier shows the same.
3. ARGS_POST IS populated for those carriers (dump rule 999905): the raw
   body is split at '&' and '=' into argument names and values
   (URL-decoded non-strictly).
4. The 2 (PL1) / 1 (PL2) payloads missed under the raw-body carriers are
   missed BECAUSE of that split: e.g. sqli-6e47f23b's text-plain body
   'admin" or "1"="1"#' splits into ARGS_POST name 'admin" or "1"' and
   value '"1"#', so 942100 (which scans single variables) does not match
   either piece, while the urlencoded carrier's body 'q=admin%22%20or...'
   decodes to one argument value and 942100 fires. Same for
   command-injection-dc2932d5 ('& dir ...' -> empty first key is dropped,
   the rest becomes one name) and command-injection-ecb32a80
   ('; type %SYSTEMROOT%\repair\SAM' -> one name, 932240 does not match).
   PROVEN by the diag10 audit entries: text/plain/octet/unknown variants
   show 999908+999905 and NO detection rule; the urlencoded variants of the
   same payloads show 999908+999905+942100/932125/932380+949110.

== C4 criterion, measured on the engine: detection blocks on a carrier do
not depend on whether its content type is on the allow-list. The engine has
NO content-type allow-list (920420 disabled by design, config 900220 sets
the allowed list to the request's own type - PROVEN from engine/config.go
and the /__kortex/config lines in docs/logs/step16-t2-runs.txt). Measured:
the engine blocks 34/40 (PL1) and 38/40 (PL2) attacks under text-plain,
octet-stream AND unknown-ct, all by detection rules
(942100/9321xx/930120/9411xx/...), i.e. detection happens on carriers the
connector refuses at phase 1 (connector: 0/40 detection blocks there - all
40 are 920420 policy blocks). The 2 (PL1) / 1 (PL2) missed payloads are a
body-PARSING fidelity gap of the URLENCODED fallback (item 4 above), not an
allow-list dependence: the engine has no allow-list to depend on, and the
same payloads ARE detected on those carriers when their encoding survives
the split.
